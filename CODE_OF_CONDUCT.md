# Community standards

T3 Factory welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. These
standards apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code; do not attack people.
- Give useful context, assume good faith and accept corrections.
- Respect people's time, privacy and stated boundaries.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, sexualize discussions, publish private
  information or repeatedly disrupt collaboration.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam or unsolicited promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Responses should reflect the
severity and repetition of the behavior, with an explanation when practical.

For sensitive incidents, use GitHub's
[private abuse reporting](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) rather than posting
personal details publicly. Do not retaliate against anyone raising a concern in good faith.

Security vulnerabilities use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).
